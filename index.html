The producer’s guide

Simple patterns.
Better sound.

1. Lock in a beat

Use the percussion circles and triangles at the bottom. Make a short pattern, then repeat it. Press Play and listen before adding more.

2. Add a melody that repeats

Click coloured squares higher up the grid. Start with a few notes. Repeat your idea, then change the final note or two. Higher squares make higher notes.

3. Choose your sound

Use the instrument controls to try different melody and drum sounds. Move the tempo slider to change the speed. Listen to the whole loop after each change.

4. Give it a lift

Try low notes below your melody. Leave a few gaps so the tune has space. Make the end slightly different, then check that it loops smoothly.

Stuck? Try this rescue recipe

Start with a blank grid and keep the default settings. Add percussion at regular intervals. Add four melody notes, repeat them, then change one note. Press Play. Keep the changes you like and undo the others.

Save it · name it · share it

Get your track into the showcase.

  1. In Song Maker, click Save, then Copy link. Save again after making further edits and copy the new link.
  2. Open your teacher’s Google Classroom assignment.
  3. Choose Add or create → Link, paste your song link, then add it. If your teacher asks for a private comment instead, paste it there.
  4. Add a track title and one sentence: “I changed ___ to make it sound ___.”
  5. Click Turn in when you are ready.

Keep the song link somewhere safe before closing the tab. This site does not collect or submit your work.
